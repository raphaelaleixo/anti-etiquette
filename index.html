Build my list How it works

Everything stays in your browser. No sign-up, and nothing is sent anywhere.

Five steps, about ninety seconds.

  1. 1

    Name wines you've drunk

    Paste them however you remember them. Loved or hated both help — a bottle you didn't finish says as much as one you did.

  2. 2

    Pick your branch

    The catalogue is read live: grape, region, price, and what is actually on the shelf at that branch.

  3. 3

    Set your price range

    Looking for a $20 bottle? You won't be shown a $100 one to impress you. The budget is a filter, not a suggestion.

  4. 4

    Take a bottle

    A short ranked list, each with a plain sentence saying why that one — built from what you already like.

  5. 5

    Want a second opinion?

    The app writes a prompt ready to paste into ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini: your taste on one side, what's in stock on the other.

No account to create.

Your list lives in your browser's storage and never leaves it. No account, no email, no analytics, no cookie banner, and nothing to delete later.

About

Anti-Étiquette matches an SAQ branch's current stock against wines you already like, and explains each suggestion in a sentence.

Your data never leaves your browser. There is no server, no database and no account — the page talks to the SAQ's own public catalogue directly, the same way saq.com does, and everything you save is stored locally. Because of that, clearing your browsing data clears your list, so the app offers an export.

This is an unofficial tool, not affiliated with or endorsed by the Société des alcools du Québec. Inventory data comes from its public catalogue. Montréal branches only.
